Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This messaging service is a small, personal-use text messaging line used for direct work communication between a limited, known set of individuals regarding property maintenance, grounds-keeping, and facility-related tasks. It is not a public or commercial messaging program.

What information is collected

We collect the mobile phone number of anyone who sends a text message to this number, along with the content of the messages exchanged.

How the information is used

Phone numbers and message content are used solely to receive, track, and respond to work-related requests and updates. Message content may be recorded in an internal tracking system for the purpose of organizing tasks.

How the information is not used

Your phone number and message content will not be sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. This information is not used for marketing, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to the work communication described above.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies and is based on the number and nature of work-related items being communicated — there is no fixed schedule. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Data retention

Message records are retained only as long as needed for the internal tracking purpose described above.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.
