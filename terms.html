Terms and Conditions — Facilities Task Messaging Line

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Program description

Facilities Task Messaging Line is a personal, low-volume SMS messaging line used for direct work-related communication between a known individual and the account holder, regarding property maintenance, grounds-keeping, and facility tasks at a shared business location. This is not a public or commercial marketing program.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and is based on the number and nature of work-related items being communicated. There is no fixed schedule.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on the recipient's mobile carrier and plan.

Support

Opting out
